Restore my subscription

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling: open Keygel → Settings → Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Cancel my subscription

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Keygel. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged.

Free trial

If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

I don't receive the reminder

Check Keygel → Settings → Daily reminder, then iOS Settings → Notifications → Keygel.

I don't feel the vibrations

Check Keygel → Settings → Haptic cues, and that vibrations are enabled in iOS Settings → Sounds & Haptics.

Reset my progress

Keygel → Settings → Reset my progress. Your sessions and streak will be deleted from this iPhone.

Wellness, not medicine

Keygel is a wellness app, not a medical device. Talk to a health professional if you feel pain or have a medical concern.
